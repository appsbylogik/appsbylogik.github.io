BrainJar

BrainJar is a clean, powerful notes app built to help you capture ideas, organize thoughts, save important details, and keep everything in one easy workspace.

Notes & Organization

More than a basic notepad. Still simple to use.

Whether you are writing quick notes, tracking ideas, saving websites, building checklists, organizing files, planning projects, or keeping private notes locked away, BrainJar gives you one simple place to store what matters.

📝

Create & Organize Notes

Create notes, organize them with folders and subfolders, and keep your ideas easy to find.

🎨

Formatting & Labels

Add bold, italic, underline, headings, bullets, numbered lists, checklists, text colors, favorites, and priority labels.

🔒

Secure Notes

Protect sensitive notes with a Secure PIN so private information stays separated and safer.

Built For Ideas

Keep your thoughts together.

BrainJar is designed for people who want more than a basic notepad, but still want an app that feels simple and easy to use. Use it for personal notes, work ideas, school notes, creative projects, saved links, lists, reminders, writing drafts, family planning, or anything else you want to keep in one place.

Features

Notes, files, links, voice, sketches, and more.

BrainJar gives you a flexible workspace for organizing information in the way that fits your life.

📁

Folders & Subfolders

Organize notes into folders and subfolders so projects, ideas, and important details stay grouped.

✅

Checklists

Build simple checklists for tasks, planning, reminders, project steps, shopping lists, and more.

🖼️

Images & Files

Add images, files, sketches, and attachments so your notes can hold more than plain text.

🎙️

Voice Notes

Record voice notes when typing is not the fastest way to capture an idea.

🔖

Websites & Bookmarks

Save websites and bookmarks with your notes so important links stay connected to your ideas.

Read-Aloud

Restore Deleted Notes

Local Backup

BrainJar Pro

More powerful tools for syncing, exporting, and backup.

Upgrade to BrainJar Pro to unlock more powerful tools for exporting, syncing, and working across devices. Pro includes no ads, cloud sync across devices, TXT exports, DOCX exports, batch exports, full folder ZIP exports, and pro export tools for better backup and organization.

Free & Pro

Start local. Upgrade when you need more.

Free users can still create and organize notes locally, use folders, checklists, formatting, secure notes, websites, attachments, and local backup. Pro is for users who want cloud sync, no ads, and advanced export tools.

☁️

Cloud Sync

Keep notes synced across devices with BrainJar Pro.

📄

TXT & DOCX Export

Export notes as TXT or DOCX files for easier backup, sharing, and long-term organization.

🗂️

Batch & Folder Exports

Batch export notes or export full folders as ZIP files with pro export tools.
