Sourdough Manager

Sourdough Manager is a simple, warm, and practical baking companion for sourdough bakers. Use it to track your starter, calculate feeding ratios, build loaf recipes, manage baking timers, follow a checklist, and keep notes or recipes in one place.

Baking Companion

A cleaner way to organize your sourdough process.

Whether you are feeding a starter, preparing a levain, planning a loaf, or timing your bake, Sourdough Manager helps keep the process organized and easier to follow.

🌾

Starter Tracking

Keep notes on your sourdough starters, feeding details, starter behavior, and baking observations.

⚖️

Ratio Calculator

Quickly calculate starter, water, and flour amounts for feeding ratios such as 1:1:1, 1:2:2, 1:3:3, and more.

🍞

Loaf Builder

Calculate flour, water, starter, and salt for one or more loaves with helpful dough total guidance.

Built For Home Bakers

Sourdough without complicated spreadsheets.

Sourdough Manager is built for home bakers who want a cleaner way to organize their sourdough process without complicated spreadsheets or scattered notes. It helps you plan, calculate, time, and document your bake from starter feeding through cooling your loaf.

Features

Everything you need for a smoother bake day.

Track your starter, calculate recipes, manage timers, follow checklists, and save notes in one place.

🧮

True Dough Totals

The Loaf Builder explains true dough totals, effective hydration, salt percentage, and prefermented flour.

🌾

Flour Guidance

Choose flour types such as store-bought white flour, bread flour, and fresh milled hard white wheat for helpful baking guidance.

⏱️

Baking Timers

Create timers for starter peak time, stretch and folds, bulk fermentation, final proofing, baking, and more.

✅

Baking Checklist

Follow a simple checklist from feeding your starter through baking and cooling your loaf.

📝

Notes & Recipes

Save sourdough notes, reminders, and recipes directly in the app so your baking details stay organized.

💾

Backup & Restore

Manually save a backup file and restore your app data later when needed.
