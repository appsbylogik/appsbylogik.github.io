CraftStock

CraftStock is an inventory and project tracking app built for makers, crafters, small shops, and handmade business owners who want an easier way to stay organized.

Inventory & Project Tracking

Know what you have, what you need, and where it is going.

Whether you work with 3D printing, vinyl, resin, leather, sewing, woodworking, laser cutting, stickers, tumblers, or other handmade products, CraftStock helps you track materials, projects, reorder needs, and inventory value in one clean workspace.

📦

Inventory Tracking

Track supplies, material quantities, categories, supplier details, SKUs, barcodes, locations, and product links.

⚠️

Low-Stock Alerts

Set reorder thresholds and quickly open a reorder list for materials that are running low.

🛠️

Project Tracking

Track active and completed projects, add materials to projects, and estimate material usage.

Built For Makers

Spend less time searching and more time making.

CraftStock is made for makers who are tired of guessing what materials they have, what needs to be reordered, and what supplies were used for each project. It gives small shops and hobby makers a cleaner way to manage inventory, projects, receipts, backups, and maker-focused workflows.

Features

A clean workspace for supplies, projects, and shop organization.

CraftStock helps you keep track of supplies, organize materials, monitor low stock, manage projects, scan receipts, create backups, and understand what you have on hand.

🏷️

Categories & Details

Organize supplies by category and add supplier, SKU, barcode, location, and product link details.

🧾

Receipts & Barcodes

Use receipt and barcode helper tools to support smoother inventory tracking and record keeping.

📋

Quick-Add Templates

Use quick-add templates for common maker supplies so adding materials is faster and easier.

📊

Insights & Reports

View insights for inventory and project tracking so you can better understand your supplies and workflow.

📤

Export & Backup

Export inventory lists, create local backups, and restore your data when needed.

🌗

Light & Dark Mode

Switch between light and dark mode with a clean maker-focused dashboard and navigation.

CraftStock Pro

Extra tools for growing maker shops.

CraftStock Pro unlocks extra tools for users who want more flexibility and fewer limits. Pro includes no banner ads, expanded inventory limits, advanced reports and insights, premium tools, and more workflow features for growing maker shops. CraftStock Pro is available as a monthly or yearly subscription through Google Play.

Who It Is For

Made for handmade businesses and creative workspaces.

CraftStock is useful for Etsy sellers, handmade shop owners, 3D printing makers, Cricut and vinyl creators, resin artists, woodworkers, leatherworkers, laser engraving shops, crafters managing many materials, and hobby makers who want better organization.

🛒

Etsy & Handmade Shops

Keep supplies, product links, reorder needs, and project materials easier to manage.

🖨️

3D Print & Laser Shops

Track filament, blanks, wood, acrylic, machine-related supplies, and project usage.

🎨

Crafters & Hobby Makers

Stay organized across resin, vinyl, leather, sewing, woodworking, tumblers, stickers, and more.

Important Note

CraftStock helps organize your information.

CraftStock is an organization and inventory tracking tool. Inventory values, project costs, low-stock alerts, and insights are based on the information you enter and should be reviewed before making business, tax, accounting, or purchasing decisions.
