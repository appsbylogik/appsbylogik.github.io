Snarky 8-Ball

Snarky 8-Ball is a magic answer app with attitude. Ask a question, shake the ball, and get a sarcastic response from a plastic oracle that is absolutely not licensed to manage your life choices.

Entertainment App

A magic 8-ball with way too much attitude.

Choose your sarcasm flavor, save your favorite answers, copy the best ones, and keep an answer history for the moments worth judging twice.

🎱

500 Funny Sayings

Get funny, sarcastic 8-ball style answers built for quick laughs, questionable decisions, and dramatic overthinking.

🌪️

Random Mode

Use Random mode when you want the full chaotic buffet across all sarcasm categories.

📱

Shake or Tap

Ask your question, then shake or tap to reveal your answer with a clean 8-ball animation.

Built For Fun

Not every app needs to fix your life.

Snarky 8-Ball is built for entertainment, laughs, and those moments when you want a silly answer from an app that acts like it knows more than it does. It will not predict the future, fix your inbox, text your ex, or make your decisions less suspicious.
