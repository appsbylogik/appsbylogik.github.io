KeepUp

KeepUp helps you stay on top of maintenance, repairs, cleaning tasks, filters, parts, and service history for the things you own.

Maintenance Tracker

Remember what needs cleaning, changing, fixing, or servicing.

Track vehicles, appliances, HVAC systems, yard equipment, tools, home systems, computers, 3D printers, and more in one organized place.

🧰

Add Items & Equipment

Create records for the things you own, including vehicles, appliances, tools, machines, home systems, and equipment.

🔁

Recurring Tasks

Create recurring maintenance tasks for filters, cleaning, oil changes, inspections, tune-ups, and other routine upkeep.

🗓️

Upcoming Service

View daily, weekly, and monthly schedules so you can see what is coming up before it gets forgotten.

Built For Real Life

A simple upkeep history for homes, vehicles, tools, and machines.

Add maintenance tasks, record completed service, attach photos, save notes, track parts and costs, and keep a clear history of what was done and when.

Features

Everything you need to keep upkeep organized.

KeepUp gives you a clean way to manage maintenance tasks, repair history, reminders, backups, photos, notes, and service records.

📋

Service History

Track maintenance and repair history so you can quickly see what was done, when it happened, and what it cost.

🔔

Reminders

Set reminders for upcoming service so filters, parts, cleaning, and routine maintenance stay on your radar.

🧾

Parts, Costs & Notes

Record parts used, service costs, notes, dates, and completion details for a clearer ownership history.

📷

Photos

Attach service-related photos when you want visual proof, receipt images, or quick reference pictures.

💾

Backup & Restore

Use local backup and restore, with optional Firebase cloud backup and restore available for Pro users.

📤

Export With Pro

Export service history with Pro so your records are easier to save, review, or share when needed.

Who It Is For

Useful for homeowners, renters, DIY users, vehicle owners, makers, and landlords.

KeepUp is for anyone who wants a simple way to remember what needs cleaning, changing, fixing, or servicing across the things they own.

Good For

Organize the things that keep life running.

From HVAC filters to vehicle service, KeepUp helps turn scattered upkeep notes into a searchable maintenance record.

Home Systems
