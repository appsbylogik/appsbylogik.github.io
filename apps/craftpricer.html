CraftPricer

CraftPricer is a pricing calculator app built for makers, crafters, Etsy sellers, 3D print shops, laser/CNC creators, and small businesses that need a simple way to price products and custom jobs.

What It Does

Pricing tools made for real craft businesses.

CraftPricer helps you calculate pricing with materials, labor, machine costs, fees, shipping, profit, and saved job details.

🧾

Job Pricing

Build pricing for custom jobs, handmade products, 3D prints, laser projects, CNC work, and more.

🧵

Materials & Costs

Track material costs, labor time, machine costs, electricity, fees, shipping, and profit margins.

☁️

Pro Cloud Backup

Optional Pro cloud backup helps save and restore CraftPricer data connected to your account.

Built For

Makers, creators, and small business owners.

CraftPricer is designed for people who sell handmade or custom products and want a cleaner, easier way to calculate prices without guessing. Whether you sell on Etsy, run a small craft business, make 3D prints, cut laser projects, or create custom work, CraftPricer helps you price with more confidence.
