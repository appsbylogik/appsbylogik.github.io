Privacy Policy

1. Overview

HustleProof is a receipt, expense, and deduction tracking app for side hustlers, small businesses, creators, gig workers, Etsy sellers, freelancers, and people who need organized proof of business expenses.

HustleProof helps users save receipts, track expenses, organize deductible and non-deductible purchases, group expenses by business or project, view summaries, search records, and export reports for bookkeeping or tax preparation.

2. Information You May Enter

HustleProof may store information you choose to enter into the app, including:

  • Email address for Firebase account sign-in
  • Receipt photos or scanned receipt images
  • Expense amounts
  • Vendor or store names
  • Expense dates
  • Expense categories
  • Payment method labels
  • Business or project names
  • Notes
  • Deductible or non-deductible status
  • Company or profile information used for report personalization
  • Local backup files
  • Cloud backup data if you sign in and use Pro cloud backup

3. Local Data Storage

Most HustleProof data is stored locally on your device. This may include receipts, expenses, categories, projects, business groups, dashboard data, report settings, profile settings, and app preferences.

If you delete the app, clear app data, lose your device, or delete local backup files, locally stored data may be lost unless you have created a backup or used cloud backup.

4. Local Backup and Restore

HustleProof may allow you to create local backup files and restore data from local backup files. Local backup files may contain receipts, receipt photos, expense records, business/project names, notes, deductible status, categories, and profile information.

You are responsible for where you save, store, upload, share, or delete those backup files. Keep backup files in a secure location and avoid sharing them with anyone who should not have access to your business or expense records.

5. Firebase Account Sign-In

HustleProof may use Firebase Authentication for email/password sign-in. If you create or use an account, Firebase Authentication may process your email address and authentication information so you can sign in, reset your password, and access cloud backup features.

Forgot password emails and authentication flows are handled through Firebase Authentication.

6. Pro Cloud Backup and Restore

If you subscribe to HustleProof Pro, sign in, and choose to use cloud backup, app data may be backed up to Firebase services. Firebase Firestore may be used for cloud backup records, and Firebase Storage may be used for receipt images.

Cloud backup data may include receipt information, receipt images, expense records, categories, business/project names, notes, deductible status, company/profile information, and related app data needed to restore your records.

7. Reports and Exports

HustleProof may allow you to export CSV and PDF reports. Exported reports may include expense totals, monthly totals, yearly totals, category breakdowns, deduction summaries, receipt details, vendor names, business/project names, notes, and company/profile information.

Once you export, save, print, email, upload, or share a report, you are responsible for protecting that exported file.

8. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

  • Save, display, search, and organize receipts and expenses
  • Create dashboards, summaries, monthly totals, yearly totals, and deduction summaries
  • Create category breakdowns, CSV reports, and PDF reports
  • Personalize reports with company/profile information
  • Provide local backup and restore
  • Provide Pro cloud backup and restore if enabled
  • Authenticate users for cloud backup
  • Process subscription status and unlock Pro features
  • Show banner ads to free users
  • Respond to support, privacy, or data deletion requests
  • Maintain app functionality, security, and compliance

9. Data Sharing

AppsByLogik does not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed by third-party services when needed to provide app features, such as Firebase sign-in, Firebase cloud backup, Google Play subscriptions, Google Play services, and Google AdMob ads.

Information may also be shared if required by law, to protect rights or safety, to prevent fraud or abuse, or to comply with app store, legal, or platform requirements.

10. Third-Party Services

HustleProof may use third-party services to provide account sign-in, cloud backup, receipt image storage, subscriptions, purchases, ads, app functionality, diagnostics, or platform services.

Third-party services may include:

  • Firebase Authentication
  • Firebase Firestore
  • Firebase Storage
  • Google Play Billing
  • Google AdMob
  • Google Play services

These services process information according to their own terms and privacy policies. You can review Google's privacy information here:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Privacy and Security
  • Google AdMob Privacy & Messaging Information

11. Data Security

HustleProof uses reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect app data. However, no method of electronic storage, cloud storage, or internet transmission is completely secure. You are responsible for protecting your device, account password, exported reports, and local backup files.

12. Data Retention

Locally stored app data remains on your device unless you delete it, clear app data, or uninstall the app. Cloud backup data may remain stored in Firebase until you delete it through app features, request deletion, or it is removed according to AppsByLogik's data practices and technical limitations.

Support emails and data deletion requests may be retained as needed to respond to your request, maintain records, prevent abuse, comply with legal obligations, or satisfy app store requirements.

13. Children's Privacy

HustleProof is intended for users who manage expenses, receipts, side hustles, small businesses, freelance work, or similar records. It is not directed to children under 13. AppsByLogik does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through HustleProof.
