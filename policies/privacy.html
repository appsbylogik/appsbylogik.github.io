Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how AppsByLogik handles information collected through this website, contact forms, support requests, and general business inquiries.

Last updated: June 10, 2026

1. Overview

AppsByLogik creates mobile apps, digital tools, creative projects, and app support pages. This website is used to share information about AppsByLogik apps, provide policy links, offer support contact options, and help users learn more about our projects.

We respect your privacy and only collect information needed to operate the website, respond to messages, support our apps, and meet legal or platform requirements.

2. Information You Provide

You may provide information when you contact us, submit a website form, request support, ask about an app, or send a privacy/data deletion request.

This may include:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • The app you are asking about
  • Your message or support request
  • Device or app details you choose to include
  • Information needed to process account or data deletion requests

3. Contact Forms

This website may use a third-party form service, such as FormSubmit, to deliver contact form messages to AppsByLogik by email. When you submit a form, the information you enter is sent to us so we can respond to your request.

Please do not submit sensitive personal information through the contact form unless it is necessary for your support or privacy request.

4. Information Collected Automatically

This website may be hosted through services such as GitHub Pages or other static website hosting. Hosting providers may automatically process limited technical information needed to deliver the site, such as IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited, and basic server logs.

AppsByLogik does not use this website to directly collect payment card information.

5. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Respond to support requests and questions
  • Provide app-related help
  • Process account deletion or data deletion requests
  • Improve website and app support information
  • Maintain security and prevent abuse
  • Comply with legal, platform, or app store requirements

6. App-Specific Privacy Policies

Each AppsByLogik app may collect and use different types of information depending on its features. App-specific privacy policies explain how data is handled inside each individual app.

Visit the Policies page for individual app privacy policies, data deletion information, and other app-related terms.

7. Third-Party Services

AppsByLogik apps and website pages may use third-party services for hosting, email delivery, contact forms, analytics, billing, ads, cloud sync, crash reporting, or app functionality. These services process information according to their own privacy policies.

Third-party services may include, depending on the app or page:

  • Google Play services
  • Google Play Billing
  • Google AdMob
  • Firebase services
  • RevenueCat
  • GitHub Pages or other hosting providers
  • FormSubmit or other contact form providers

8. Data Sharing

AppsByLogik does not sell your personal information.

Information may be shared or processed only when needed to operate the website, deliver contact messages, provide app support, comply with legal requirements, protect security, or use service providers that support website or app functionality.

9. Data Security

We use reasonable steps to protect information submitted through the website and support channels. However, no method of online transmission or electronic storage is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

10. Data Retention

Support messages, contact form submissions, and privacy request emails may be kept as long as needed to respond to the request, maintain records, prevent abuse, comply with legal obligations, or support app store requirements.

11. Your Choices and Rights

You may contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of information you have submitted through this website, where technically and legally possible.

If your request involves data inside a specific app, include the app name and the email address used for that app account, if applicable.

12. Children’s Privacy

This website is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through this website. If you believe a child has submitted personal information, contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When changes are made, the updated policy will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
