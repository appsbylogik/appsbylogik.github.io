CraftStock is an inventory, project, and maker-business organization app designed to help users track materials, projects, categories, low-stock items, receipts, exports, backups, and related workflow details.

These policies explain how CraftStock handles privacy, data, subscriptions, ads, backups, support, and general terms of use.

1. Privacy Policy

Information CraftStock May Store

CraftStock may allow you to enter, save, or manage information such as:

  • Inventory item names
  • Categories
  • Quantities
  • Units of measurement
  • Low-stock thresholds
  • Material costs
  • Supplier names
  • SKU numbers
  • Barcode values
  • Product links
  • Storage locations
  • Project names
  • Customer names
  • Customer contact information
  • Project notes
  • Project material usage
  • Receipt-related information
  • Backup files
  • Exported inventory files
  • App settings and preferences

This information is used to provide the app’s main features, including inventory tracking, project tracking, low-stock reminders, reorder lists, receipt helper tools, barcode helper tools, exports, backups, and insights.

Local Data Storage

CraftStock is designed to keep most app data stored locally on your device unless you choose to export, back up, share, or send that data somewhere else.

If you create a backup file, export a CSV, share a file, send an email, or open a link, that action may move information outside the CraftStock app into another app, service, email provider, file storage provider, or sharing destination chosen by you.

You are responsible for protecting exported files, backup files, and any information you share outside the app.

Camera, Photos, Files, and Device Permissions

CraftStock may request access to certain device features only when needed for app features. These may include:

  • Camera access for barcode scanning or receipt capture
  • Photo or file access for selecting receipt images, importing backups, or saving/exporting files
  • Storage or file picker access for backup and restore tools
  • Notification permission for reminders or low-stock alerts, if supported on your device

CraftStock uses these permissions only to provide the feature you are trying to use.

Receipt OCR and Barcode Tools

CraftStock may include receipt OCR helper tools and barcode helper tools. These features may process receipt images or barcode information to help you enter inventory details faster.

OCR and barcode results may not always be perfect. You should review all extracted text, prices, quantities, SKUs, barcodes, and item details before saving or relying on them.

Advertising

Free users may see banner advertisements inside CraftStock.

CraftStock may use Google AdMob or similar advertising services to display ads. These services may collect or use device information, advertising identifiers, approximate location, app activity, diagnostics, or other data according to their own privacy policies and settings.

CraftStock does not sell your personal inventory or project data to advertisers.

CraftStock Pro users should not see banner ads while Pro access is active.

Purchases and Subscriptions

CraftStock Pro subscriptions are handled through Google Play Billing.

CraftStock does not directly collect or store your credit card number, bank details, or full payment information. Payment processing, subscription management, cancellations, renewals, and payment methods are handled by Google Play.

CraftStock may receive basic purchase status information from Google Play so the app can unlock Pro features.

Third-Party Services

CraftStock may use or interact with third-party services, including but not limited to:

  • Google Play Billing
  • Google AdMob
  • Android system services
  • Email apps
  • File picker apps
  • Sharing apps
  • Browser apps
  • Backup or storage apps selected by the user

When you use a third-party service, that service may collect and process information according to its own privacy policy.

Children’s Privacy

CraftStock is not intended for children under 13. Do not knowingly submit personal information from children under 13 through the app.

Data Deletion

Because CraftStock stores most data locally on your device, you can delete your data by:

  • Deleting individual inventory items, projects, categories, or records inside the app
  • Clearing app data from your device settings
  • Uninstalling the app
  • Deleting any backup or export files you created

If you shared, emailed, uploaded, or backed up data outside the app, you must delete that data from the destination where you saved or sent it.

For help, contact: AppsByLogik+craftstock@gmail.com

Data Security

CraftStock is designed to help organize your information, but no app, device, or storage method is guaranteed to be completely secure. You are responsible for securing your device, backups, exported files, and any accounts or services you use to store CraftStock data.
