Sourdough Manager Policy

Official policy and support information for AppsByLogik apps.

Sourdough Manager is designed to store most app data locally on your device. AppsByLogik does not automatically receive or store your starters, notes, recipes, timers, checklist progress, or calculator settings.

Information Stored Locally

The app may store the following information on your device:

  • Starter names and starter details
  • Timer names, durations, and timer status
  • Baking checklist progress
  • Notes and recipes
  • Calculator preferences and settings
  • Supporter/remove-ads status
  • Backup and restore data that you choose to create or import

This information is used only to provide the app’s features.

Backup and Restore

Sourdough Manager includes a manual backup and restore feature. When you choose to save a backup file, the app creates a backup file containing your app data. You choose where to save that file.

AppsByLogik does not automatically upload, receive, or store your backup files. You are responsible for where you save your backup files and who you share them with.

When you choose to restore a backup file, the app reads the file you selected so it can restore your saved app data.

Ads

The free version of Sourdough Manager may show ads using Google AdMob.

Google AdMob and related Google services may collect or process information to provide, measure, limit, and improve ads. This may include:

  • Advertising ID
  • Device information
  • Approximate location or ad-related location data
  • App interaction data related to ads
  • Diagnostics and performance data
  • Crash or error-related data connected to ad delivery

Ad data is handled by Google according to Google’s own policies and your device settings.

Remove Ads Purchase

Sourdough Manager offers a one-time purchase to remove ads.

Purchases are processed through Google Play Billing. AppsByLogik does not collect or store your full payment card number, bank information, or Google Play payment details.

The app may receive purchase confirmation information from Google Play so it can unlock supporter status and remove ads.

The remove-ads product ID is: remove_ads

Email Support and Feedback

If you contact AppsByLogik by email, we may receive the information you choose to send, such as your email address, name, support question, feedback, screenshots, or files.

This information is used only to respond to your message, provide support, troubleshoot problems, or improve the app.

Please do not send sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for your support request.

Website and External Links

Sourdough Manager may include links to external websites or services, including the AppsByLogik website, email app, PayPal, Cash App, Google Play, or other third-party services.

When you open an external link, you are leaving Sourdough Manager. Third-party services have their own privacy policies and practices. AppsByLogik is not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or security of third-party websites or services.

Data Sharing

AppsByLogik does not sell your personal information.

Limited information may be processed by third-party services used by the app, including:

  • Google AdMob for ads
  • Google Play Billing for purchases and restore purchase checks
  • Your email provider if you contact support
  • External apps or websites if you choose to open links from the app

Your locally stored notes, recipes, starters, timers, and checklist data are not automatically sent to AppsByLogik.

Permissions

Sourdough Manager may request or use permissions needed for app features, such as:

  • Notifications, so timers can alert you when they finish
  • File picker or storage access, so you can save and open backup files
  • Internet access, for ads, Google Play purchases, support links, website links, and related services

Permissions may vary depending on your Android version and device settings.

Data Security

Sourdough Manager stores app data locally on your device. Backup files are only created when you choose to create them.

Because backup files may contain your app data, you should store them in a safe location and avoid sharing them with others unless you are comfortable sharing that information.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, but the app is designed to limit collection by keeping most user-created data local to your device.

Children’s Privacy

Sourdough Manager is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children.

The app does not include account registration, social features, direct messaging, or public user-submitted content.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to AppsByLogik through email or support, please contact us at appsbylogik@gmail.com and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

Data Retention and Deletion

Most app data is stored locally on your device.

You can delete local app data by:

  • Deleting notes, recipes, timers, starters, or checklist progress inside the app where available
  • Clearing the app’s data through Android settings
  • Uninstalling the app
  • Deleting any backup files you created

Support emails may be retained as needed to respond to support requests, keep records, prevent abuse, or improve the app.

If you want to request deletion of information you sent directly to AppsByLogik by email, contact: appsbylogik@gmail.com

Changes to This Privacy Policy

AppsByLogik may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When changes are made, the updated policy will be posted on the AppsByLogik website or the app’s privacy policy page. The effective date should be updated when changes are published.
