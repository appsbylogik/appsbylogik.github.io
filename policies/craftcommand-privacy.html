1. Privacy Policy

CraftCommand is a business organization app designed for makers, crafters, handmade sellers, and small business owners. The app helps users manage customers, orders, expenses, payments, reports, quotes, invoices, business information, and related records.

Information You May Enter Into the App

CraftCommand may allow you to enter, save, or manage information such as:

  • Customer names
  • Customer contact details, such as phone numbers, email addresses, or notes
  • Order details
  • Product or project descriptions
  • Order pricing, payment status, balances, and due dates
  • Business expenses
  • Business profile details
  • Quote and invoice details
  • Images, design references, receipt photos, or attachments if you choose to add them
  • Backup, export, and report files created by the app

This information is entered by you and is used to provide the features of the app.

Local Data Storage

CraftCommand is designed to store app data locally on your device unless a cloud sync, backup, or online feature is added or enabled in a future version. Local data may include customers, orders, expenses, business profile information, reports, invoices, quotes, settings, and attachments.

If you delete the app from your device, locally stored data may also be deleted unless you have created and saved a backup separately.

Backups, Exports, Quotes, Invoices, and Reports

CraftCommand may allow you to create backups, export data, generate reports, create quotes, or create invoices. These files may contain the business or customer information that you entered into the app.

You are responsible for deciding where to save, send, store, or share exported files, invoices, quotes, backups, and reports. Once you share or save those files outside the app, they may be handled by other apps, services, email providers, cloud storage providers, or recipients.

AppsByLogik does not sell your personal information, customer information, order details, business records, or app data.

CraftCommand may use app store tools, device tools, or third-party services to receive basic diagnostic information, crash reports, performance information, or usage-related data. This type of information helps improve app stability, fix errors, and understand app performance.

Advertising

CraftCommand may include advertisements in free versions of the app. Ads may be provided by third-party advertising networks, such as Google AdMob or similar services.

Advertising providers may collect or process information such as device identifiers, ad interactions, approximate location, diagnostics, and usage information according to their own policies. Users may be able to limit personalized advertising through their device settings.

CraftCommand may offer paid features, subscriptions, or Pro upgrades. Purchases and subscriptions are processed through the app store, such as Google Play. AppsByLogik does not directly collect or store your full payment card information.
