Privacy Policy

1. Overview

KeepUp is a maintenance, repair, cleaning, and service tracking app for things users own. It helps users organize recurring upkeep tasks, service history, parts, receipts, notes, and reminders for home systems, vehicles, appliances, yard equipment, tools, machines, computers, 3D printers, and other personal property.

KeepUp lets users track what needs to be maintained, cleaned, changed, repaired, or serviced, and keeps a history of completed work.

2. Information You May Enter

KeepUp may store information you choose to enter into the app, including:

  • Email address for Firebase account sign-in
  • Item names and categories
  • Vehicle details, such as year, make, model, and VIN
  • Home system, appliance, tool, machine, computer, or equipment details
  • Filter sizes, model numbers, serial numbers, locations, and other item details
  • Maintenance task types and recurring maintenance schedules
  • Service, repair, cleaning, and maintenance history
  • Notes
  • Parts used
  • Costs
  • Dates and completion details
  • Receipt/photo labels or attached service-related images, depending on feature use
  • Local backup files
  • Cloud backup data if you sign in and use Pro cloud backup

3. Local Data Storage

Most KeepUp data is stored locally on your device. This may include items, categories, item details, maintenance schedules, recurring task settings, completed service history, notes, parts used, costs, dates, photos, labels, app settings, and preferences.

If you delete the app, clear app data, lose your device, or delete local backup files, locally stored data may be lost unless you have created a backup or used cloud backup.

4. Local Backup and Restore

KeepUp may allow you to create local backup files and restore data from local backup files. Local backup files may contain item details, schedules, maintenance history, notes, parts, costs, dates, receipt/photo labels, service-related images, and settings.

You are responsible for where you save, store, upload, share, or delete those backup files. Keep backup files in a secure location and avoid sharing them with anyone who should not have access to your property, vehicle, equipment, or service records.

5. Firebase Account Sign-In

KeepUp may use Firebase Authentication for email/password sign-in. If you create or use an account, Firebase Authentication may process your email address and authentication information so you can sign in, reset your password, and access cloud backup features.

Forgot password emails and authentication flows are handled through Firebase Authentication.

6. Pro Cloud Backup and Restore

If you subscribe to KeepUp Pro, sign in, and choose to use cloud backup, app data may be backed up to Firebase services. Firebase Firestore may be used for cloud backup records, and Firebase Storage may be used for service images, receipt images, or other attached images if image backup is enabled.

Cloud backup data may include item information, category details, maintenance schedules, service history, notes, parts used, costs, dates, completion details, receipt/photo labels, service-related images, and related app data needed to restore your records.

7. Reminders, Calendar Views, and Service History

KeepUp may use your entered schedules and completed task details to show upcoming maintenance in agenda, weekly, or monthly calendar views. KeepUp may also allow you to search service history and export service history.

Reminder dates, recurring schedules, and calendar views are based on information you enter and app logic. They may not reflect official service intervals or manufacturer requirements.

8. Exports

KeepUp may allow you to export service history or maintenance records. Exported files may include item names, categories, item details, maintenance schedules, completed tasks, parts, costs, dates, notes, and service details.

Once you export, save, print, email, upload, or share a file, you are responsible for protecting that exported file.

9. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

  • Save, display, search, and organize items you own
  • Create and manage maintenance task types by category
  • Schedule recurring maintenance, cleaning, repair, or service tasks
  • Show upcoming maintenance in agenda, weekly, or monthly calendar views
  • Save completion details when tasks are marked done
  • Track service, repair, cleaning, and maintenance history
  • Store notes, parts used, costs, dates, and service details
  • Export service history
  • Provide local backup and restore
  • Provide Pro cloud backup and restore if enabled
  • Authenticate users for cloud backup
  • Process subscription status and unlock Pro features
  • Show banner ads to free users
  • Respond to support, privacy, or data deletion requests
  • Maintain app functionality, security, and compliance

10. Data Sharing

AppsByLogik does not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed by third-party services when needed to provide app features, such as Firebase sign-in, Firebase cloud backup, Google Play subscriptions, Google Play services, and Google AdMob ads.

Information may also be shared if required by law, to protect rights or safety, to prevent fraud or abuse, or to comply with app store, legal, or platform requirements.

11. Third-Party Services

KeepUp may use third-party services to provide account sign-in, cloud backup, image storage, subscriptions, purchases, ads, app functionality, diagnostics, or platform services.

Third-party services may include:

  • Firebase Authentication
  • Firebase Firestore
  • Firebase Storage
  • Google Play Billing
  • Google AdMob
  • Google Play services

These services process information according to their own terms and privacy policies. You can review Google's privacy information here:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Privacy and Security
  • Google AdMob Privacy & Messaging Information

12. Data Security

KeepUp uses reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect app data. However, no method of electronic storage, cloud storage, or internet transmission is completely secure. You are responsible for protecting your device, account password, exported files, and local backup files.

13. Data Retention

Locally stored app data remains on your device unless you delete it, clear app data, or uninstall the app. Cloud backup data may remain stored in Firebase until you delete it through app features, request deletion, or it is removed according to AppsByLogik's data practices and technical limitations.

Support emails and data deletion requests may be retained as needed to respond to your request, maintain records, prevent abuse, comply with legal obligations, or satisfy app store requirements.

14. Children's Privacy

KeepUp is intended for users who manage maintenance, repair, cleaning, service, and property records. It is not directed to children under 13. AppsByLogik does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through KeepUp.
